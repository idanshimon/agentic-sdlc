10
The differentiator

Ask every vendor this follow-up

"When your agent decided alone, how often was it wrong?"
Never 0%. A rate computed from too few samples is not a weak signal — it is a false one.

We refuse to report

Below the sample floor a class returns INSUFFICIENT, never 0%.

Unknown is never agreement

Decisions never scored return UNSCORED and are excluded, rather than quietly counted as the agent having been right.

Thresholds live in your versioned bundles, not our code. Changing what counts as acceptable divergence is a governed standards change with a canary week — not a redeploy by us.

11
Credibility

What we got wrong

We shipped a rule marked BLOCK with no scanner behind it. SBOM-001 reported clean because nothing was ever scanned.

An audit artifact pretending to be a control is the failure mode to watch for — in our product and in everyone else's.

If a vendor cannot name the mechanism behind a control, they have a spreadsheet of intentions.

12
Honest limits

Find them here, not in a pilot

Identity not enforced end-to-end

AUTH_MODE=entra refuses to run rather than fake JWT validation, and app auth is off on the demo. Team boundaries are advisory until closed.

Delivery not wired for every path

Some runs end at "Delivery blocked — synthetic provider output." The governance story is real; full autonomous delivery is not the claim.

Precedent is thin

16 pairs. The learning loop is demonstrably working, not demonstrably mature.

Rule binding is at the gate

Ledger rows cite the autonomy policy; binding each row to the bundle rule version is the next increment, not a shipped claim.
